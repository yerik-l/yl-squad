Prepare your videos once.
Publish to TikTok when you're ready.

YL Imagination Squad Automation is a web studio for photographers and content creators. Keep your finished short videos in one queue, preview them, write the caption, choose who can see each post, and publish to your own TikTok account with one click.

Open the Studio

1. Connect TikTok

Sign in with TikTok. We only ask for permission to read your basic profile and to post videos you choose.

2. Pick a video

Upload a video or choose one from your prepared queue and watch the preview before anything is sent.

3. You decide the settings

Write the title, pick the privacy level, allow or block comments, Duet and Stitch, and disclose promotional content.

4. Publish

Nothing is posted until you press Publish. Your video appears on your TikTok profile after TikTok finishes processing it.
