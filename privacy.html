Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains what YL Imagination Squad Automation (the "Service") collects when you use it, and why.

What we collect

  • From TikTok, after you sign in: your TikTok open ID, display name, username and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and access/refresh tokens that let the Service post only the videos you choose (scopes video.publish and video.upload).
  • Posting options: the posting options TikTok returns for your account (allowed privacy levels, whether comments, Duet and Stitch are available, maximum video length).
  • Videos you upload or queue: stored so TikTok can retrieve them when you press Publish.

We do not collect your TikTok password, we do not read your messages, followers or other videos, and we do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

How we use it

  • To show which TikTok account you are posting to.
  • To show the posting options that are allowed for your account.
  • To publish a video to your TikTok account, only after you press Publish, and to show you the publishing status.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone other than TikTok (to publish your post) and our hosting provider (Supabase, which stores the data on our behalf).

How long we keep it

  • Tokens are kept while your account is connected and are deleted when you press Disconnect.
  • Videos are kept only as long as needed for publishing and are deleted within 30 days.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time in the Studio or in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). To ask us to delete any remaining data, message us on TikTok at @ylimaginationsquad.

Children

The Service is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will update the date above if this policy changes.
